Eons ago, there was the %#F878FF%<shake>Architects' <shake>Prism%#%. It was originless - it had no maker, no beginning, constantly at the boundary between common sense and everything that is imaginary. Its only purpose - shaping thoughts into a raw form, and then taking this form and bringing it to reality. Creating something out of nothing, defying all natural laws.

The %#F878FF%<shake>Prism%#% was discovered and wielded by the greatest nation of its time, millennia before the present day. Its power gave rise to elegant weapons of incredible power, cities of unimaginable beauty, and studies pursuing the deeper understanding of the universe itself. The %#F878FF%<shake>Prism%#% helped shape different magical arts, as well as the creation of other artifacts (although less powerful) that still exist today.

However, should the thoughts fueling the %#F878FF%<shake>Prism%#% ever become any %#6206A1%corrupt%#%, its creations would %#6206A1%twist%#% and %#6206A1%turn dark%#%. The wisest sorcerers of the nation quickly realized this and formed a Council of Mystic Arts, devoted to protecting the miraculous artifact from ill hands, while ensuring everything it brings into the world stays pure and of good intentions.

One day, a curious sorcerer decided to experiment with The Prism’s true capabilities. It started innocently, by creating a few more items, each one stronger than the previous one. But the sorcerer was unsatisfied with the results, and decided to push the artifact further... Within a day, horrible things happened across the entire nation. The dead rose from their graves and started to roam the lands. Crops started wilting, tainted by the darkness. People started to fall ill to mysterious sickness never seen before. The last test resulted in the creation of The Command Block, an object of such uncontrollable power it immediately went rogue. It gave birth to creations and beings so powerful, yet so terrible, that they consumed their creator and destroyed the wielding nation overnight. Everything the nation achieved was reduced into mere ashes, to be blown away by the wind.

The remaining members of the Council barely managed to contain the outbreak of the chaos, but the aftermath already happened. Recognizing the danger coming from The Prism’s powers, they resolved to destroy it. The Command Block resisted every spell, seemingly indestructible. The Council managed to put it in a dormant state, never to be used again.

Though The %#F878FF%<shake>Prism%#% was shattered into millions of pieces, its essence survived, slipping between the threads of the fabric of reality. With time, it started to slowly manifest back, adapting the form of Eyes. Every Eye inherited a tiny fraction of the Prism's powers, but some were scattered across realms, some lost to time, and for the rest, nobody is sure their fate. Knowledge of them withered - first becoming legends, and eventually fading into nothing more than myths.

Yet there remains an old saying that whoever retrieves all of the Eyes can restore the %#F878FF%<shake>Architects' <shake>Prism%#% and wield its miraculous powers of creation. Throughout history, many tried to prove the myths are true and attempted to find the Eyes. To this day, nobody succeeded in this journey.
